Questions

Quick answers.

Do I need any editing skills?

No. You type an idea and approve the script and characters. ClipGleam does the filming, voices, music and edit.

How long does a video take?

A short AI clip takes about 5–10 minutes. One minute of full AI video takes about 20–40 minutes. Template videos are quicker.

Is there a subscription?

No. You buy a credit pack from £5 and use it when you like. Credits last 12 months.

Can I keep the same characters in every episode?

Yes. Each character gets one face and one voice, locked for the whole series.
