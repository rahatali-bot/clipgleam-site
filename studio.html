The Studio

One idea in.
One finished video out.

Seven steps, all automatic. You type once and approve along the way. Here is how the Studio will work.

Step 1

The idea box

Everything starts with one sentence. A paragraph or a full script works too.

  • Add a reference: a picture, a script, a voice or a song
  • Pick format, style, language and length
  • See an honest estimate of credits and waiting time before you spend anything

Step 2

Script editor

ClipGleam writes the full script in scenes. Read it, change a line, or rewrite one scene without touching the rest.

  • Quick changes: shorter, more dramatic, add a twist
  • Plain-language edits: "end on a cliffhanger"
  • Nothing is made until you approve the script

Step 3

Characters & cast

Every character gets one face and one voice, locked for the whole video. The same character in every scene, every episode.

  • Auto-cast from your script
  • Hear each voice before you approve
  • Upload your own face or voice, only with consent

Step 4

Storyboard & camera plan

Every shot is planned the way a film director would plan it, before anything is rendered.

  • Shot size, lens, angle and movement for every frame
  • Change one shot and only that frame redraws
  • Play the animatic to check the pacing first

  • Live checklist: script, voices, characters, scenes, music, edit
  • Every shot is scored before it is accepted
  • A weak shot is retaken automatically
