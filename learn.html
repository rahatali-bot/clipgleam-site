FAQ

Is ClipGleam open now?

We are in early access. Leave your email and we'll invite you as soon as your place is ready.

Do I need a subscription?

No. Buy credits once and use them when you want. They last 12 months.

Who owns the videos?

You do. Use them on your own channels and for your business.

Can I keep the same character across episodes?

Yes, that's what the cast lock is for: one face and one voice, kept the same in every scene.

Which languages?

English first. More voices and languages are on the way.

Can I upload my own face or voice?

Yes, but only your own, or someone who has given their permission.
